# Northstar Software: Headcount and Compensation Policy (FY2027)

Fictional policy for the workshop. Each rule has an id so a copilot can cite it.

## Requests

**POL-HC-01 Required fields.** Every headcount request names the role title, level (L3 to L7), location, start quarter, request type (net-new or backfill) and a business justification of at least two sentences: what work it unblocks and what happens if the role is not filled.

**POL-HC-02 Plan fit.** A net-new request must fit the team's approved FY2027 headcount plan for its start quarter: approved net-new for that quarter minus reqs already opened must be at least one. A request outside the plan needs CFO approval and either an offsetting reduction or new budget.

**POL-HC-03 Backfills.** A backfill for someone who left in the last 90 days skips the plan check, but still goes to the Finance Business Partner.

**POL-HC-04 Lead time.** Recruiting needs 60 days. The start date must be at least 60 days after the request is approved. The default start date is the first day of the start quarter's second month.

## Compensation

**POL-COMP-01 Bands.** The proposed base salary must sit inside the band for the level and location tier. Proposing above the band midpoint needs a one-line reason (competing offer, scarce skill, internal parity). Above the band maximum needs a Compensation Committee exception and is out of scope for this workflow.

**POL-COMP-02 Location tiers.** New York and San Francisco are Tier 1. Seattle, Boston and Austin are Tier 2. Remote (US) is Tier 3. A role may only be hired in a location where the team already has an office, or Remote (US).

## Budget

**POL-FIN-01 Budget fit.** The FY2027 impact of the hire (fully loaded annual cost prorated by months employed in FY2027, plus one-time costs) must fit inside the team's remaining FY2027 personnel budget (budget minus committed).

**POL-FIN-02 Cost model.** Fully loaded annual cost = base + target bonus + annual equity + benefits (18% of base) + employer taxes (8% of base). One-time costs are internal recruiting ($15,000, or 20% of base if an agency is used) and equipment ($4,000).

## Approvals

**POL-APR-01 Chain.** Every request goes to the department head, then the Finance Business Partner.

**POL-APR-02 CFO.** The CFO is added when the fully loaded annual cost is above $350,000, when the level is L7, or when the request is outside the plan (POL-HC-02).

**POL-APR-03 Decisions.** An approver can approve, reject, or send back with a comment. A request sent back keeps its id and returns to the requester with the comment attached.
